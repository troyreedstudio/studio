# Let Me Check — Revenue Forecast & Unit Economics

> Locked-in reference for the deck + fundraising. Rebuilt 2026-08-02 with Troy on the
> **urgency-based pricing model**: you pay for *how fast you want it*, not for the type of
> check. Three speed tiers — **Instant · Fast · Standard** — with add-ons stacked on top.
> **Illustrative forecast** — a defensible model, not a guarantee. The one dial that drives
> everything: **checks/day per city.**

---

## 1. Pricing model — priced by urgency, not check type

One clean axis: **how fast do you want it?** Works for any check (a club, an airport, a
DMV line, a rental) — the customer self-selects, and urgency naturally tracks how much
they're willing to pay. The Scout is paid **more for speed** (drop everything = earn more).

| Tier | Delivery | Seeker pays | Scout earns | **LMC gross margin** |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Instant** | ≤ 5 min | $20.00 | $12.00 | **$8.00** |
| **Fast** | ~ 8 min | $15.00 | $8.00 | **$7.00** |
| **Standard** | ~ 10–12 min | $10.00 | $6.00 | **$4.00** |

**Blended** (assumed mix **30% Instant / 40% Fast / 30% Standard**):

| | Blended |
|---|---:|
| Seeker pays (gross booking) | **$15.00** |
| Scout earns (paid out) | **$8.60** |
| **Our gross margin** (take after Scout pay) | **$6.40** |
| Variable platform cost (Mux + Stripe ~3% + infra) | **~$1.20** |
| **Net contribution / check** | **~$5.20** |

*Two revenue framings: **gross bookings** = what Seekers pay ($15.00); **our gross profit** =
the platform's take after paying the Scout ($6.40). The deck leads with gross profit — it's what LMC keeps.*

### Add-ons (stack on top of any speed tier)

| Add-on | Seeker pays | Scout earns | **LMC keeps** | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **Vibe Check** — 30-sec interior look | +$5.00 | +$2.00 | **+$3.00** | Partner venues; ~25% attach on experience checks |
| **Line Pass** — hold your spot + prepaid tab | $50–300 | + bonus | **10–20%** | Venue queue commerce; a check becomes a booking |

## 2. Aggressive ramp — 7 US cities to 1,000 checks/day each

Launch order (Miami is the wedge, then the top US metros):
**Miami → New York → Los Angeles → Atlanta → Chicago → Houston → San Francisco.**

Demand is **influencer-led / viral**, and cities switch on instantly (data, not code) — so the
ramp is fast: **all 7 cities live in Year 1 → 1,000 checks/day each by Year 2** (7,000/day,
~2.56M checks/yr). Break-even is just **~100 checks/day per city.**

The cheaper **Standard ($10)** tier is the volume lever — it makes LMC a low-friction daily
habit (a DMV line, a gym, a store, a beach) rather than a special-occasion splurge, which is
what pushes checks/day per city up toward — and past — 1,000.

| | Year 1 (launch + ramp) | Year 2 (run-rate) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Avg checks/day per city | ~250 (ramping) | **1,000** |
| Total checks / year | ~640K | **~2.56M** |
| Gross bookings / year | ~$9.6M | **~$40M** |
| **LMC gross profit / year** | **~$4.1M** | **~$19.5M** |

## 3. Where the revenue comes from — itemized at the 1,000/day run-rate

Not a blended hand-wave — every line is **volume × price**. Mix is premium-skewed: we steer
demand toward **Instant** in the high-spend moments, while **Standard** builds the daily volume.

| Revenue stream | Volume / yr | Bookings (or GMV) / yr | Gross profit / yr |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Instant check** — $20 · ≤5 min | 767K · 30% | $15.3M | $6.1M |
| **Fast check** — $15 · ~8 min | 1.02M · 40% | $15.3M | $7.2M |
| **Standard check** — $10 · ~12 min | 767K · 30% | $7.7M | $3.1M |
| *Base checks subtotal* | *2.56M* | *$38.3M* | *$16.4M* |
| + Vibe Check add-on — +$5 · ~25% attach | 256K | $1.3M | $0.8M |
| + Line Pass — ~$150 tab · ~15% take · ~10% of experience checks | 102K | $15.3M *(GMV)* | $2.3M |
| **Total · 7 US cities @ 1,000/day** | **2.56M checks** | **~$55M** | **~$19.5M** |

**At the 1,000-a-day target the 7 US cities run at ~$55M gross bookings / ~$19.5M gross
profit/yr.** That is *before* the prepaid-credit float engine and *before* any international city.

## 4. Market sizing — TAM / SAM / SOM

The opportunity in investment terms. We are the first mover in a new category that sits inside
the experience economy ($9T travel, $900B restaurants, $500B gig).

| Market | Basis | Size / year |
|---|---|---:|
| **TAM** — total addressable | 130M US on-demand users · ~1 check/mo · $15 | **~$23B** |
| **SAM** — serviceable | 7 launch metros · 66.8M people · ~26M on-demand users | **~$4.7B** |
| **SOM** — Year-2 target | 2.6M checks · ~0.8% of SAM captured | **~$40M** |

At the Year-2 target we hold **under 1% of the serviceable market** — the rest is the growth
runway, and global is a multiple of the US.

## 6. What this does NOT yet include (upside above the base check)

- **Prepaid-credit float + breakage** and the **instant-payout fee** (Starbucks + Venmo model). (See `MONETIZATION-STRATEGY.md`.)
- **First-check-free acquisition** — Uber's playbook. LTV/CAC is strong: a ~3-check/month user is worth ~$230/yr gross profit; a free first check costs us ~$12. (Acquisition line, not steady-state revenue.)
- **International expansion** — next wave, English-speaking first: **London · Sydney · Melbourne · Dubai · Singapore** — additive on top of the US.

The base check builds the habit and covers costs; the Line Pass + float layer is the venture-scale business on top.

---

## 7. Dials to adjust

1. **Checks/day per city** — the single biggest lever (§5 sensitivity table).
2. **Tier mix** — 30/40/30 assumed; steering demand toward Instant lifts the $6.40 blended margin.
3. **Scout take** — launch-generous (Uber's lesson: don't squeeze the supply side early); can tighten with scale.
4. **Ramp speed** — Year 1/2 here; real ramp per city is slower in months 1–3 as Scout supply densifies, then accelerates with influencer-led demand.
