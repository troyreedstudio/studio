**Owner:** Troy

---

## The one-line thesis

> **LMC is the only way to show a real place, right now, that can't be faked.**
> The consumer check is the wedge. **Venues are the business.**

Every other form of venue marketing — Google photos, Instagram, influencers, "as seen on" — is **staged, old, or paid-for-praise.** LMC's footage is **GPS-stamped, timestamped, third-party-filmed, and real-time.** That's a brand-new advertising format that doesn't exist anywhere else. Venues won't pay for a review. They'll pay for **proof.**

## The reframe: who the real customer is

| | Role | Margin |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer check ($15–20) | **Loss leader** — builds the habit + the Scout network | Thin |
| **Venue relationship** | The real business — advertising, subscriptions, enterprise | **Fat + recurring** |

We are not choosing between consumers and venues. **Venues subsidise consumers.**

---

## The money math (this is the important part)

**Consumer-paid check (today):**
- Seeker pays **$15** (+$1.50 fee) → Scout earns **$8** → **LMC keeps ~$7**

**Venue-sponsored check (the unlock):**
- Venue pays **~$12** → Scout earns **$8** → **LMC keeps ~$4** → **consumer pays $0**

**The key insight:** when a venue sponsors the check, the "loss leader" *isn't a loss.* The consumer gets it free, but **the venue covers the Scout's pay AND leaves LMC a margin.** So venue money lets us **give consumers free checks at a profit** — that's how we buy explosive consumer growth without burning cash. The loss-leader is free *to the consumer*, profitable *to us*.

## The flywheel

```
Venue spend  →  free / cheap consumer checks  →  consumer growth
     ↑                                                   ↓
venue value  ←  denser Scout network  ←  more checks, more demand
```

Venue dollars fund consumer acquisition **at a margin**; consumer growth makes venue placement more valuable. Defensible loop.

## The four venue plays (build order) + pricing

> All numbers are **illustrative v0.2** — we anchor them to *what a walk-in is actually worth* per venue type (a restaurant cover nets ~$10–25; a gym lead is worth ~$20–50; a beach-club minimum spend is $50–200). Refine against real venue conversations.

### 1. Sponsored / free checks — the loss-leader engine 🔁
A venue **pays so anyone can check it free.** *"See [venue] right now — free, on us."* They're buying authentic, real-time foot-traffic advertising.
- **Price:** ~**$10–12 per sponsored check** at volume (between Scout cost $8 and consumer retail $15). Sold as **monthly pools.**
- **Example:** a slow-Tuesday restaurant funds **50 checks/mo = ~$600/mo** (LMC margin ~$200). Cheaper than one boosted Instagram post, and it actually puts people at the door.

### 2. Partner Program — recurring B2B subscription 💳
Monthly SaaS, **per location:**

| Tier | Price/mo | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| **Listed** (default) | Free | Basic pin, exterior checks only |
| **Partner Lite** | **$99** | Interior-check unlock (the +$5 30-sec, already built), **Verified badge**, control of reference photo, monthly analytics |
| **Partner Pro** | **$299** | + Priority Scout dispatch, live analytics dashboard, Vibe-feed eligibility, **10 sponsored checks/mo included** |
| **Partner Flagship** | **$699** | + Featured Vibe placement, **40 sponsored checks/mo**, dedicated support |

Analytics is the hook: *"checked 340× this month, peak Fri 11pm, 4.7★ vibe."* Subscription revenue is near-pure margin (software).

### 3. Live "Vibe" feed — FOMO discovery & ad placement 🔥
A real-time feed: *"What's buzzing near you right now."*
- **Price:** **Boost packs ~$149–499/week** for a featured slot in the local feed (or CPM ~$25 per 1,000 views once volume justifies it).
- This is the **discovery layer** that gets people opening LMC when they aren't already checking a specific place.

### 4. Enterprise B2B ops — the quiet giant 🏢
Multi-location brands pay LMC to **check their own locations** (line length, display setup, cleanliness, compliance) — **mystery-shopper-as-a-service.**
- **Price:** **~$6–9 per check** at volume + a **platform/reporting fee ($500–2,000/mo).**
- **Example:** a gym chain, 30 locations, checked 2×/week → ~240 checks/mo + reporting → **~$30k–60k/yr contract.**
- Same Scout network, sold to **head office.** Slow to sell, but sticky and fat. **This may be the real money.**

## The one rule that protects everything: authenticity

**Venues buy visibility, never praise.**
The instant a venue can pay to fake, stage, or hide a clip, the moat dies.

> *"You can't control what the clip shows — only whether people see your good moments."*

That honesty is **why consumers trust it** and **why the ad format beats Instagram.** Protect it ruthlessly: no staged/venue-supplied footage in the verified feed; clips stay GPS + signage verified; a venue can *sponsor* and *boost*, never *edit reality.*

## Go-to-market: the first 5 Miami venues

1. Pick **5 venues that want foot traffic on slow nights** (a restaurant Tue–Wed, a new bar, a mid-week beach club, a boutique gym, a hotel bar).
2. Offer **30 days of sponsored free checks, no charge.**
3. Let them *feel* the foot traffic + see the analytics.
4. Convert to Partner subscription + paid sponsored-check pools.

One move = **consumer acquisition** (free checks) **and** the **venue proof-of-concept.**
