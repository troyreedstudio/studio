# Let Me Check — Monetization & Market Strategy

> Working strategy doc. Captured 2026-06-25 from product conversations between Troy and Guy.
> Covers three linked ideas: (1) broadening the use cases, (2) tiered pricing for frequency,
> (3) the prepaid-credits "float + breakage" profit engine (the Starbucks / Venmo model).

---

## 0. The core idea

LMC answers one question:

> **"What's it *actually* like there right now?"** — for anything a photo, a website, or
> Google can't tell you, because the answer changes by the minute.

Nightclubs (the Miami launch wedge) are just *one* version of that question. The use case,
not the venue, is the business. The strategy below is about widening LMC from a niche
nightlife app into a **daily-use utility** — and then monetizing the habit, not just the check.

---

## 1. Positioning — the core need (the spine everything hangs off)

**Diagnosis:** "Visual verification on demand" is a *capability*, not a *need*. Uber isn't
"driver dispatch" — it's *"get me there."* Uber Eats isn't "courier logistics" — it's
*"I'm hungry now."* LMC must name the **itch**, not the mechanism.

**The itch (the universal wish):** everyone, everywhere, constantly has had this exact thought —

> **"I wish someone could just go look for me."**

You've thought it about a restaurant, a beach, an apartment, a parking lot, your storefront,
your parents' house after a storm. Nobody has ever been able to *buy* that. **LMC turns that
universal wish into a button** — the first time it's ever existed.

**The "why now" (the aha, and the moat):** in the AI era you **can't trust anything you see
online anymore** — listings faked, reviews bought, photos old/staged/AI-generated ("the
pictures looked nothing like it"). LMC is the antidote: a **real human, right now, giving you
ground truth you cannot fake** (live, GPS- and time-stamped). As fake images explode, the one
thing getting *rarer and more valuable* is **proof that something is real, on demand.**
- "Pics or it didn't happen" → **"Let me check."**
- The moat: anyone can fake a photo; nobody can fake a *live human clip of your specific
  question at this minute.*

**Why it becomes a hit — the two-speed engine** (a hit needs a reason to download AND to stay):
- **Download (WOW / install trigger):** a *high-stakes* moment — the sight-unseen rental you
  fear is a scam, the hour drive you refuse to gamble, the storm-check on a loved one's home.
  Big emotion, money/time on the line. *"A real person showed me in 8 minutes"* → instant
  believer + word of mouth.
- **Stay (habit):** once they realize they have **eyes everywhere**, the *small everyday*
  checks kick in — busy? open? line? worth it? — the daily reps that make it a verb.

People come for the scary big moment; they stay for the everyday small ones.

**The reframe:** LMC is not a places app. **It sells presence and truth** — the power to be
somewhere you're not, and to *know it's real* before you commit time, money, or trust.

**Tagline candidates:** "Eyes everywhere. On demand." · "Ground truth. In minutes." ·
"See it before you go. Trust it before you pay." · "Someone to go look — for you."
*Lead with the emotional hook ("I wish someone could just go look for me") + the rational
why-now ("real eyes, real-time, can't be faked").*

## 2. Broadening the market — what people can "check"

Grouped by the question the user is really asking.

### A. "How long's the line / how busy is it?" — the killer recurring category
DMV, post office, passport & courthouse offices · airport security / immigration ·
Apple Store / Genius Bar · banks · popular restaurants & brunch (table wait) ·
food trucks / viral bakeries · pharmacies, urgent care, ER · theme-park ride lines ·
concert / event entry · **voting lines on election day**.

### B. "Is it worth going right now?" — vibe / crowd / conditions
Beaches (crowded? clean? seaweed? parking?) · gyms (how packed *before* you drive there) ·
parks, trails, dog parks · **surf & ski conditions** (surfers/skiers already pay for this) ·
farmers/flea markets, festivals · cafés / libraries / co-working (free seats? quiet? wifi?).

### C. "Is it real / as described?" — high-value remote decisions (people pay a LOT here)
**Apartment & rental viewings** (remote: students, expats, city-movers) · **used cars**
before a long drive · **vacation rentals / villas** (is the Airbnb actually like the photos —
acute pain in places like Bali) · Facebook Marketplace / Craigslist furniture, boats, RVs ·
wedding / event venue scouting from afar · real estate & neighborhood feel.

### D. "Did it happen / is it okay?" — proof & peace of mind
Package on the porch? · storefront / second home okay (small biz, expats, snowbirds) ·
is the contractor actually working today · **parking: spots in that lot right now** (huge in cities).

### E. "What's the situation?" — status / conditions
Flooding or storm damage on a street · road / bridge open after weather · power back on
(lights on?) · is that beach club / restaurant actually open today (hours that lie).

**Two natures of check** (this drives pricing):

| | Frequent / low-stakes | Occasional / high-stakes |
|---|---|---|
| Examples | gym busy?, DMV line, beach crowded | apartment viewing, used car, villa check |
| Will pay | a few dollars, or won't bother | $15–50 easily (decision worth thousands) |
| Plays to | **daily habit / frequency** | **margin / serious utility** |

## 3. Pricing — tier it, don't flatten it

**Keep $15–20, but don't make it the *only* price.** A single high price keeps LMC an
*occasional* app; a single low price cheapens the brand AND breaks Scout economics.

Recommended shape:

1. **Quick Check (~$5–7)** — a fast glance (line, crowd, "is it open"). Drives the daily habit.
2. **Standard Check ($15)** — the current 15-sec clip. The core.
3. **Premium / Detailed ($20–40+)** — interiors, walkthroughs, real estate, cars. The real margin.
4. **Credits / subscription** — see §4. Frequency without per-tap friction.

**Does lowering cheapen the brand? No — *if* tiered.** Tiered pricing reads as *smart*, not
cheap (UberX vs Black; standard vs express shipping). The price reflects the job.

**Hard constraint — the Scout floor.** A Scout must earn enough to bother (~$8–12 today).
You can't slash to $3. You get cheap *quick* checks by (a) making them genuinely quicker
(5-sec glance = less Scout time = lower pay = lower price), or (b) selling **credits in bulk**
so each one *feels* cheap while the brand stays premium.

## 4. The profit engine — prepaid credits (Starbucks float + Venmo idle balance)

The deeper model (from the Starbucks/Venmo breakdown): **the product creates the habit, the
app holds the money, and the float + breakage is the business.** Starbucks earns on ~$1.75B
of customers' money before a single latte is poured; Venmo earns on your idle balance.

### How it maps to LMC

**Prepaid Credits = LMC's float** — users load a balance ("$25 of checks", "10-pack")
instead of paying per check. This delivers four things at once:
- **Float** — LMC holds the balance and earns on it before any check is redeemed.
- **Breakage** — credits bought but never used = pure margin (no Scout to pay).
- **Cash upfront** — money in *before* payouts (cash-flow positive).
- **Stickiness** — a loaded balance is a sunk cost; users return to "use it up".

**LMC has TWO float pools** (one more than Starbucks):
1. Seeker prepaid credits (above).
2. **Scout earnings sitting in-app before withdrawal** — the *Venmo idle-balance* pool. The
   **instant-payout 2% fee** (already planned, see OUTSTANDING.md) is exactly how Venmo/PayPal
   monetize idle money. LMC is already doing this move.

**The reframe:** LMC stops being "a cut per check" and becomes a **balance-holding habit
engine**. Per-check is the on-ramp; float, breakage, instant-payout fees — and eventually a
tiered loyalty program (Starbucks Green/Gold/Reserve → LMC Seeker/Scout tiers) or a
co-branded card — become the deeper money.

### Honest caveats (go in clear-eyed)
- **Scale play, not day-one revenue.** Starbucks's float comes from 35M members; at 1,000
  users LMC's float is pocket change. Build the *mechanism* now, expect the *money* at scale.
- **Holding customer money is regulated** (stored-value / money-transmitter rules vary by
  state & country). Cleanest path: structure as **non-refundable service credits** (lighter
  regulation than cash-equivalent balances), and/or use a banking-as-a-service partner.
- **Breakage has consumer-protection rules** in some places (can't expire; unused balances may
  escheat to the state). Design deliberately, with legal input.

## 4b. Subscription tiers (v1, locked 2026-08-02)

Built + hidden in `lmc-app/app/(seeker)/membership.tsx`, re-tuned to the urgency pricing
(Instant $20 / Fast $15 / Standard $10). **Casual users prepay credit; power users subscribe** —
subscriptions are the recurring-revenue (MRR) engine investors reward.

| Plan | Price | Included / month | Perks | Our margin at full use |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| **Pay-as-you-go** | $0 | Pay per check ($10/$15/$20) | — | per check |
| **LMC Plus** ⭐ | $29/mo | 3 Fast checks (~$45 value) · 10% off extras | Scheduled checks, unlimited saves, keep clips, priority support | ~$5 + breakage |
| **LMC Pro** | $69/mo | 5 Fast + 1 Instant (~$95 value) · 15% off extras | + priority dispatch, 4K, concierge, API | ~$17 + breakage |

**Design rule:** per-check margin is thin ($4 Standard / $7 Fast / $8 Instant after Scout pay),
so plans are sized to be **profitable even if every included check is used** — breakage is a bonus,
not a crutch. The old "$29 for 10 checks" plan lost money at full redemption (the ClassPass trap).

**Status:** pulled from V1 (Stripe subscription setup + not day-one). Screen hidden (Apple rejects
unhooked features), ready to switch on. Numbers are a tunable first version.

### Acquisition funnel (first-check-free → prepaid)

1. **First check free** — get them in the door (~$8–10 Scout cost = cheap CAC).
2. **Load $50, get 2 more free** (3 total) — converts to a prepaid, card-captured, habitual user;
   LMC now holds the $50 float. Make the free ones **Standard** (cost control) and **launch-only /
   first-timer** so the giveaway doesn't outrun the economics.

---

## 5. Bottom line

- **Widen the use cases** beyond nightlife — position LMC as "know what it's *actually* like
  there, right now, for anything." The launch stays tight (Miami nightlife); the product is universal.
- **Tier the pricing** (Quick / Standard / Premium) — daily-habit app AND high-value utility.
- **Adopt prepaid credits** — solves frequency/pricing AND hands LMC the Starbucks-float +
  Venmo-idle-balance profit engine. Already halfway there via the instant-payout fee.
- Treat the **financial layer** (float, breakage, instant-payout, future loyalty/card) as a
  real revenue stream — built early, monetized at scale.
