How to permanently delete your Let Me Check account and the data associated with it.

Let Me Check (operated by Black Malibu Inc.) lets you delete your account and associated personal data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Let Me Check app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and associated personal data are removed (subject to the limited records below).

Option 2 — Request by email

Email hello@letmecheckapp.com from the email address (or with the phone number) on your account, with the subject “Delete my account.” We verify it's you and complete the deletion, then confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, phone number, and display details.
  • Your saved places, recent searches, ratings, and check history tied to your account.
  • Any video clips associated with your account.

What is kept, and for how long

A limited set of records is retained only where we are legally required to, or to prevent fraud and meet payment/tax obligations:

DataRetention
Transaction & payment records (held by our payment processor, Stripe)As required by law (typically up to 7 years), then deleted.
Fraud / abuse-prevention and security logsUp to 24 months, then deleted.

All other personal data is deleted promptly on request and on a rolling basis thereafter. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
