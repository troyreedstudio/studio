## Who We Are

Let Me Check is operated by Black Malibu Inc. ("we", "us", "our").
We connect Seekers (people who want a live video check of a real location) with
Scouts (independent contractors who film that location on demand).

Contact us: help@letmecheck.com

---

## What Information We Collect

### 1. Account information

When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive:

- Your name (as shared by Apple or Google)
- Your email address

We use this to create and maintain your account.
We do not receive your Apple or Google password.

### 2. Your location

When you use the app, we collect your precise GPS location.

- **Seekers:** We use your location to show you nearby venues and to find a Scout
  close to where you want a check filmed.
- **Scouts:** We use your location to match you with nearby job requests. Your
  location is shared with the Seeker's app for the duration of an active check.

Location data is collected only while the app is open and you have granted
location permission. You can revoke this permission in your iPhone Settings at
any time, but the app will not be able to find nearby Scouts or jobs without it.

### 3. Videos filmed by Scouts

Scouts film short video clips (typically 15 to 30 seconds) at the requested
location using their device camera.

- The video is uploaded to our video hosting provider (Mux -- see Third Parties
  below).
- The video is delivered to the Seeker who requested it.
- Faces in videos are automatically blurred before the video is delivered, to
  protect the privacy of people who appear in the footage.
- Videos are stored for a limited period and are not sold or shared beyond the
  parties to the check.

If you appear in a video and want to request its removal, contact us at
help@letmecheck.com.

### 4. Push notification token

If you grant notification permission, your device generates a push notification
token. We store this token to send you alerts about your check (for example,
"Your Scout is filming now" or "Your check is ready to watch").

We do not use push tokens for advertising. You can turn notifications off in
iPhone Settings at any time.

### 5. Payment information

Payments between Seekers and Scouts are processed by Stripe. We do not store
your card number, CVV, or full card details on our servers.

What Stripe processes on our behalf:
- Card authorization and capture at the time of a check request
- Payout transfers to Scouts via Stripe Connect

Stripe's own privacy policy applies to payment data:
https://stripe.com/privacy

We retain a payment record (amount, status, timestamp) in our database for
financial reconciliation and to show you your transaction history. This record
does not contain your card details.

### 6. App usage and technical data

We collect basic technical data required to operate the service:

- The check status and timeline (requested, assigned, delivered, etc.)
- Ratings you give Scouts (1 to 5 stars)
- Saved places and recent checks you add in the app

We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not collect
crash reports through a crash-reporting tool (crash reports come through Apple's
standard App Store crash logs, which we review via App Store Connect).

## How We Use Your Information

| Information | Why we use it |
|-------------|---------------|
| Name and email | Account management, sign-in, support |
| Precise location | Matching Seekers with nearby Scouts; Scout job dispatch |
| Videos | Delivering the requested check to the Seeker |
| Push token | Sending check-status notifications |
| Payment record | Transaction history, financial reconciliation |

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or AI model training.

---

## Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell your personal information.

We share data only with service providers that help us operate the app:

| Provider | What they receive | Why |
|----------|-------------------|-----|
| Supabase (Supabase, Inc.) | Account data, check records, location data | Database and authentication infrastructure |
| Mux (Mux, Inc.) | Videos filmed by Scouts | Video upload, processing, and delivery |
| Stripe (Stripe, Inc.) | Payment processing, Scout payouts | Payment infrastructure |
| Expo (Expo Technology, Inc.) | Device push token | Push notification delivery |

Each provider processes data according to their own privacy policy and our data
processing agreements with them.

If required by law or to protect our users' safety, we may disclose information
to law enforcement or regulatory authorities.

## Data Retention

We keep your account information and check history for as long as your account
is active, or for as long as required by applicable law for financial
reconciliation.

When you delete your account (see below), we remove your personal information
from our active database. Financial records (payment amounts, dates, and
transaction IDs) may be retained in an anonymized form for legal and accounting
purposes, as permitted by law.

---

## Deleting Your Account

You can permanently delete your Let Me Check account from within the app:

1. Open the app
2. Go to your Profile
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account"
4. Confirm when prompted

Deleting your account removes your profile, saved places, recurring checks, push
token, and all personal data from our systems. Financial records linked to
completed payments are anonymized (your identity is removed, but the transaction
record is kept for accounting) in line with standard financial record-keeping
requirements.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

## Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data (see "Deleting Your Account" above)
- Object to or restrict our processing of your data

To exercise these rights, email us at help@letmecheck.com. We will respond
within 30 days.

## Children

Let Me Check is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided
us with personal information, contact us at help@letmecheck.com and we will
delete it promptly.

## Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including
encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls on our database. No
method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you believe your account
has been compromised, contact us immediately at help@letmecheck.com.
