## 3. Seeker Terms

### What you are paying for

When you submit a check request, you are paying a Scout (an independent
contractor, not a Let Me Check employee) to physically travel to a location
and film a short video from a public area. You are not paying for a guaranteed
result -- if no Scout is available or the Scout cannot reach the location in
time, you will not be charged.

### Payments

- Payments are processed by Stripe at the time of your request.
- Your card is authorized (held) when you submit a request. The charge is
  only captured when your video is delivered.
- If no Scout accepts or the request times out, the authorization is released
  and you are not charged.

### Refunds

You may request a refund from within the app within 24 hours of delivery if:

- The video is too blurry to see clearly
- The Scout filmed the wrong location
- The video was never delivered within the agreed time

Refunds are processed by Stripe. Scout earnings are not reversed by a Seeker
refund -- the Scout is compensated regardless.

## 4. Scout Terms

### You are an independent contractor

Scouts are independent contractors, not employees of Black Malibu Inc. You
set your own schedule, choose which requests to accept, and use your own
device. Let Me Check does not direct or control your work beyond setting the
check request parameters (location, timing, and length).

As a contractor, you are responsible for your own taxes and compliance with
local laws.

### Filming rules

By accepting a check request, you confirm:

- You will only film from publicly accessible areas.
- You will not enter private property without permission.
- You will not film people in private situations or in a way that would
  violate their reasonable expectation of privacy.
- You will not film in locations where filming is prohibited.

The app automatically blurs faces in your videos before delivery. Do not
attempt to defeat or bypass this.

### Payouts

Scout earnings are paid via Stripe Connect. You must complete Stripe's
Know Your Customer (KYC) verification before you can go online and accept
requests. Stripe's terms apply to your Connect account and payouts.
