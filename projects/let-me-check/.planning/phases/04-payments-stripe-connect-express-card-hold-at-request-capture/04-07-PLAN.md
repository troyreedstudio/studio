<objective>
Build the reason-coded, reviewed refund flow (D-06/D-07/D-08, PAY-04/PAY-05). The `stripe-refund` edge records a structured refund request, runs a pure auto-approval rule (first refund in the window auto-approves; repeat refunders go to manual review), and — on approval — refunds the Seeker against the platform charge WITHOUT reversing the Scout's transfer, so the Scout always keeps their pay. The delivery screen gets a "report a problem" reason picker that calls it. There is NO no-questions self-refund (deferred).

Purpose: Refunds are an abuse surface and a Scout-trust surface. Structured reasons feed quality/fraud signals (event log), automated review limits abuse, and never-reversing the transfer is the concrete mechanism behind "Scout keeps pay" (D-08). Bad/fake clips are NOT handled here — that is the Phase-5 verification gate (D-08a).
Output: stripe-refund edge + its Deno test, the pure refund-rules module, and the delivery-screen reason picker.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| client → stripe-refund | A Seeker may only refund their own delivered check |
| refund decision → money | Auto-approval is rule-bound; repeats require a human |
| refund → Scout transfer | A refund must never reverse the Scout's pay (D-08) |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-04-31 | Elevation of Privilege | refund someone else's check | mitigate | Ownership check (check.seeker_id === caller) -> 403 (test 2); refund_requests RLS limits INSERT to own seeker_id |
| T-04-32 | Tampering | refund abuse / repeat refunders | mitigate | evaluateRefund: first-in-window auto, repeats -> manual_review (no auto money); per-Seeker 30d count |
| T-04-33 | Tampering | Scout pay clawback on refund | mitigate | refunds.create NEVER includes reverse_transfer (grep gate + test 5) — platform absorbs (D-08) |
| T-04-34 | Repudiation | refund reason | mitigate | Every request stores reason_code immutably; log_event 'payment.refunded'/'payment.refund_flagged' |
| T-04-35 | Tampering | client-chosen refund amount | mitigate | Amount derived server-side from the payment row; client supplies only checkId + reason (no amount field) |
| T-04-36 | Information Disclosure | refund of an uncaptured/unknown check | mitigate | Server validates a captured payment exists (except never_delivered) before refunding |
</threat_model>
