| T-09-02 | Information Disclosure | get_check_scout_public on undelivered check | mitigate | Status gate: only `delivered`/`rated` checks expose a scout; pgTAP throws_ok enforces it. |

| T-09-04 | Tampering | profiles UPDATE of another user's prefs/cities | mitigate | Existing profiles_update_own RLS (0005) scopes updates to `id = auth.uid()`; new columns inherit the row-level policy. No new policy needed. |
